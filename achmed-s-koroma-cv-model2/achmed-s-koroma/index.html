About

About Me

I am a PhD candidate working on AI-driven, closed-loop automation for nanomaterials discovery. My background combines a bachelor’s degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Fourah Bay College, University of Sierra Leone, with an International MSc in Engineering Technology for Strategy and Security from the University of Genoa.

At the Italian National Research Council (CNR-IEIIT) I worked as a research intern and then as a research fellow on explainable and reliable AI, adversarial machine learning and perception for robotics, which led to a 2025 journal publication. In Sierra Leone I taught STEM subjects for a decade and worked as an electrical engineer on solar PV and industrial systems.

I am interested in AI that is trustworthy and useful in the real world — in the lab, in robotics and in education. Alongside research, I create digital content: short videos, flyers and graphics made with tools such as Canva, OBS Studio and AI image generators. My goal is to contribute to research and engineering teams building reliable, explainable AI for science and society.
